# UK Sponsorship Jobs — Design draft

This is a **private design prototype**, not a publicly deployed job board.

## Preview
Open `index.html` in a browser. This self-contained demonstration includes sample job cards and embedded example data so no server is required.

## What it shows
- A restrained one-column job-search page without sidebars.
- Search and one sponsorship-status dropdown.
- Three separate evidence questions: job advert wording, sponsor licence and previous Certificate of Sponsorship activity.
- Direct links to employer adverts.
- Clear 'not independently verified' wording where sponsor records are missing.

## Important limitations
The four listings are examples researched on 22 October 2026. Their current vacancy status has **not** been verified continuously. Historical employer CoS counts are **not** populated. Do not market this prototype as a live verified service.
